Homeroom

Homeroom periods are taken out of the teacher schedules and matched with the homeroom class in the TIMESHEET. Each class should have one homeroom teacher. Homeroom periods overlap class lessons by design, and they are not counted in the Timesheet hours.

What each role can do

Viewer: opens the saved workbook of a semester and campus and sees every tab. Cannot change anything.

Registrar: also chooses files, saves them to a semester, adds semesters and sees the saved list.

Admin: also arranges the schedule by drag and drop, deletes saved workbooks and manages users here.

The person signs in with the username and password you give them. Accounts can be disabled but not deleted, so saved work stays linked to a name.

Campuses

These are the choices in the Campus box at the top of every page. Each campus keeps its own saved workbook inside a semester. The campus name is read from the workbook (for example "CKD Campus" or a file name like 11_CKD_...).
